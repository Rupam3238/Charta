LicenseRef-Okular-upstream-terms

Redistributor's position for the KDE Okular translation catalogues shipped in po/.

These files are copied verbatim from KDE Okular (https://invent.kde.org/graphics/okular). No
catalogue carries an SPDX-License-Identifier, and only 265 of the 1 069 carry an
SPDX-FileCopyrightText line naming a translator; the remaining 804 carry neither. Upstream has no
REUSE.toml and no .reuse/dep5, so there is no machine-readable grant for them in either project.

STATUS: INTERIM CLASSIFICATION ONLY. It is not a grant, not a permission, and not evidence that
the upstream copyright holders licensed these files for redistribution on these terms. The
maintainer's decision of 2026-10-21 was to accept this identifier as an interim classification
while the question stays open, and to make no claim of permission until KDE clarifies the
redistribution terms.

This file records the position taken by the redistributor, not a grant made by the upstream
copyright holders:

  * The files are redistributed unmodified from upstream.
  * The upstream project as a whole is licensed GPL-2.0-or-later, and the catalogues are shipped
    as part of that project.
  * The upstream maintainers and KDE l10n were asked to state the terms explicitly. The question,
    its status, and the evidence are recorded in docs/licensing/translations.md.
  * Until that answer arrives, this identifier is a placeholder that says "the upstream terms
    apply, and here is what we currently believe them to be" - it does not assert them, and it
    does not discharge anyone's obligations. No licence identifier is asserted for these files
    beyond the placeholder, and the translator copyright lines are preserved exactly as upstream
    has them.

If the upstream answer differs from the above, this file and
docs/licensing/translations.md must be updated in the same change.
